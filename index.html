Welcome to Kegel Timer

This timer guides you through one or two sets of pelvic floor exercises, with adjustable contraction, relaxation, and recovery intervals.

How to Use

Open Exercise settings to customize your routine, then press Start.

Each set begins with a five-second preparation countdown. Follow the audio signals:

  • High-pitched beep: Contract your pelvic floor muscles.
  • Low-pitched beep: Relax your muscles.
  • Two beeps: The current set is complete.
  • Three beeps: The second set is about to begin, followed by a five-second preparation countdown.

Your settings are saved automatically for your next session.
